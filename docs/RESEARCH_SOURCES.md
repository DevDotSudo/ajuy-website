- Iloilo Provincial Government, Municipality of Ajuy directory: `https://iloilo.gov.ph/en/node/137`

- Philippine census references collected in the Ajuy population table: `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajuy,_Iloilo`

The PSA profile is the primary source for the 2024 population, number of barangays, urban or rural classification, and PSGC codes. The website presents historical census observations instead of inventing annual population estimates.
