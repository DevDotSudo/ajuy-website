# Content Guide

## Update rules

- Use official government sources for statistics, officials, services, and contacts.
- Record a review date for information that can change.
- Do not publish unverified emergency numbers.
- Confirm tourism access, prices, schedules, boat routes, and private business details with the responsible operator or municipal office.
- Review history and cultural writing with residents, elders, culture workers, and the municipal office.
- Use images with clear ownership, license, alternative text, caption, and source.
- Do not treat Google Maps pins as proof of an exact entrance, route, property boundary, or service status.
- Keep chatbot knowledge limited to approved public information.

## Attraction records

Each entry in `data/attractions.ts` should include:

- Unique slug
- Official or commonly verified place name
- Category
- Image and useful alternative text
- Clear description
- Visitor highlights
- Safety or verification notice
- Google Maps search query
- Barangay or area
- Source note
